How it works

Getting started

  • Add it to your home screen (share icon → Add to Home Screen) so it opens like a real app.
  • Set your daily goals for water, protein, and calories in Settings.
  • Turn on notifications under Remind if you want reminders through the day.

Logging something

  • Tap Log under Water, Protein, or Calories on the main screen.
  • Pick a quick amount, or enter a custom one and adjust the time if you're catching up on something from earlier.
  • Sleep works differently — tap Log Sleep and enter Lights Out and Woke Up times; hours are calculated for you, including overnight sessions that cross midnight. Naps use the same button and add to the day's total.
  • Made a mistake? Tap the trash icon next to the entry in Today's log.

Presets — one-tap logging

  • In Settings, add anything you log often, with its amount.
  • It shows up as a button on the main screen — one tap, no typing.

Reports

  • Switch between Water / Protein / Cal / Sleep, or pick "All 4" to compare them at once.
  • "All 4" shows each as a percentage of its own goal, since the units don't match.
  • Switch between Day / Week / Month, and use the arrows to look back.

Reminders

  • Push notifications: real alerts even with the app closed. Turn on in Remind.
  • Bedtime reminder: a separate, once-a-day push at whatever time you want to start winding down.
  • In-app nudge: only works while the app is open on screen — a lighter backup.
  • Calendar file: a one-time download that adds recurring reminders to your phone's calendar.

Backing up your data

  • In Settings → Backup, export a backup before switching phones or reinstalling.
  • Import it on the new device to bring your history back.
